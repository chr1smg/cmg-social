What the Numbers on a Radiator Valve Mean
Not a temperature, not a volume dial — the numbers set how warm the room gets before that radiator shuts itself off.
1
Turning it to 5 doesn't heat the room faster. It just lets the room get hotter before the radiator stops — a target, not an accelerator.
2
Different rooms want different numbers. Bedroom on 2–3, living room on 4 — heating an empty spare room to sitting-room warmth is money through the wall.
3
One radiator often has no valve by design — usually where the main thermostat lives. Head won't turn? Don't force it — engineer's job.
