MYTH VS FACT
“It's Cheaper to Leave the Heating On Low All Day”
MYTH
Keeping it ticking over on low all day and night costs less than turning it on and off.
FACT
A warm house leaks heat all the time. Fewer warm hours means less gas — for most homes, a timer set to real life wins.
VS
The bigger saving is the controls: a thermostat that works, a timer set to how you actually live, and radiator valves turned down in rooms nobody sits in.
