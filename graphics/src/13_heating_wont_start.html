Heating Not Coming On? Check These First
Four two-minute checks before you ring anybody — they sort it more often than you'd think.
Is anything asking for heat? Programmer on, right mode, and the thermostat set higher than the room already is.
The pressure gauge. Most boilers want around 1 to 1.5 bar cold — below that, plenty simply refuse to fire.
Thermostat batteries. Not a joke — one of the most common causes of “the heating's dead” there is.
The switch and the fuse. The fused spur by the boiler gets knocked off during decorating more than anyone admits.
Fault code, losing pressure, water, or a smell of gas? Stop — Gas Safe engineer. Smell gas: 0800 111 999 first.
